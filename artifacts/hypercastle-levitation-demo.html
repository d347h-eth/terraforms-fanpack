The Hypercastle vertical levitation: 3650-day triangular z-oscillation

The onchain formula moves levels through a 10-year cycle. The whole structure reaches an upper peak near year 2.5, returns to baseline near year 5, reaches a lower trough near year 7.5, then returns to baseline at year 10. Upper levels are intensified during the upward half; lower levels are intensified during the downward half.

This visualizes only the zOscillation term. The vertical plate view uses the real per-level dimensions from terraforms-structure.ts, with the oscillation magnified so it can be seen against the much larger level spacing. Browser JS does not continuously recompute the terrain heightmap.
